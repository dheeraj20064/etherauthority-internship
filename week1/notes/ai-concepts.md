## 1. What is Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human 
intelligence in machines that are programmed to think, 
learn, and make decisions like humans.

AI systems can perform tasks such as:
- Understanding natural language
- Recognizing images and speech
- Making decisions and predictions
- Solving complex problems

### History and Evolution of AI
- 1950 — Alan Turing proposed the Turing Test
- 1956 — Term "Artificial Intelligence" coined at 
  Dartmouth Conference
- 1980s — Expert systems became popular
- 1990s — Machine learning gained momentum
- 2010s — Deep learning revolution began
- 2020s — Large Language Models (ChatGPT, Claude) emerged

---

## 2. AI vs ML vs Deep Learning

| Concept | Definition | Example |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| AI | Machines simulating human intelligence | Chess playing computer |
| ML | Machines learning from data without being explicitly programmed | Spam email filter |
| Deep Learning | ML using neural networks with many layers | Image recognition, ChatGPT |

Think of it like circles:
- AI is the biggest circle
- ML is inside AI
- Deep Learning is inside ML
